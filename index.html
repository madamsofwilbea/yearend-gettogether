Before you register
  1. Members only, and you must be 18 or over. The Grand Barkadahan is for Madams of WilBea members — no guests or plus-ones. You may register up to five people in one go, as long as every one of them is a member too.
  2. Your slot is only yours once we've verified your payment. Registering doesn't hold a place by itself. The Circle checks every payment, then emails you — that email is your slot.
  3. Come dressed for movement. There will be games, and some of them involve actually moving. Comfortable clothes and rubber shoes, please — save the heels for the Gala.
  4. Photos and videos will be taken and posted. We'll be documenting the day for our recap posts, so by registering you're happy to appear in them.
  5. No refunds and no transfers. Your ₱1,000 goes straight into the venue, the food, the game materials and prizes, and your loot bag — all booked and bought ahead of the day. Once it's in, we can't return it or pass your slot to someone else, so please be sure before you pay.
